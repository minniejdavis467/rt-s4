RTPS Bihar: Complete Guide to Online Certificates and Government Services

Rtps 4 is one of the most searched terms among Bihar residents looking for online government services such as caste certificates, income certificates, residence certificates, birth certificates, and other public service applications.

The RTPS Bihar portal has transformed the way citizens access government services by making the entire process digital, transparent, and time-saving.

RTPS stands for Right to Public Service, a major initiative launched by the Government of Bihar to ensure that citizens receive important services within a fixed time frame.

What is RTPS Bihar?

RTPS Bihar is an e-governance platform designed to provide public services online. The portal is integrated with Service Plus Bihar and allows citizens to submit applications, track status, and download certificates digitally.

The primary aim of this system is to reduce corruption, increase transparency, and provide faster services to the public.

Services Available on RTPS Bihar

  • Caste Certificate
  • Income Certificate
  • Residence Certificate
  • EWS Certificate
  • Character Certificate
  • Birth Certificate
  • Death Certificate

Main Features of RTPS Bihar

1. Online Application

Citizens can apply for certificates online using the Service Plus Bihar portal.

2. Application Status Tracking

Users receive a reference number that can be used to track application status online.

3. Digital Certificate Download

Approved certificates can be downloaded digitally in PDF format.

4. Transparent Process

The RTPS system helps reduce delays and improves transparency.

5. Time-Saving Services

Citizens no longer need to repeatedly visit government offices.

How to Apply for Certificates on RTPS Bihar

Step 1: Visit the Official Portal

Open the RTPS Bihar or Service Online Bihar portal.

Step 2: Select the Service

Choose the certificate or service you want to apply for.

Step 3: Fill the Application Form

Enter personal details such as name, address, Aadhaar details, and contact information.

Step 4: Upload Documents

  • Aadhaar Card
  • Passport-size photo
  • Address proof
  • Supporting documents

Step 5: Submit the Form

After submission, an application reference number is generated.
